## Reddit r/Albuquerque cadence

**Pre-work (weeks 1–2):** Don't post your site. Comment helpfully on others' "what to do this weekend" threads. Build 50+ karma.

**Weekly:**
- **Thursday 8 AM MT** — "ABQ Weekend Roundup [Date]" — 5–7 hand-picked events, native Reddit format, link at bottom.
- **Sunday 7 PM MT** — "Free things to do this week" — curated free events only.
- **Monthly (1st)** — "First Friday Crawl Map" — Old Town/Nob Hill/Downtown art crawl summary.

**Post format:**

```
ABQ Weekend Picks — Apr 25–27

Friday
- Sunshine Theater: [Band] 8pm, $25
- Marble Brewery: Trivia 7pm, free

Saturday
- KiMo: [Show] 7:30pm
- Old Town: Spring Market 10am–4pm, free

Sunday
- Launchpad: Punk matinee 4pm, $15

Full list: abqunplugged.com/weekend
```

**Rules:** Max 1x/day. Reply to comments within 4 hours. Disclose ownership in profile bio.

## Tracking

After backlinks land, check **GSC → Links → External links** to confirm Google sees them. Review monthly. Drop targets that don't produce a measurable referral after 60 days.

The Reddit weekend cadence is your single most reliable channel — never skip it. A bad week of stats is fine; a quiet week is what kills aggregators.

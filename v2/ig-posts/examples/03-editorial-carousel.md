# Example 3 — Editorial Carousel (5 slides)

**Style:** Multi-slide carousel mixing photo, quote cards, stats, and call-to-action. Tells a story, not a single statement.

**Best if:** You want max dwell time + saves. Carousels are IG's highest-performing format in 2024 — average 1.8x the engagement rate of single-image posts (Social Insider, 2024). Perfect for "sell me on this event I've never heard of."

---

## The 5 Slides

### Slide 1 — The Cover (STOP-SCROLL)

**Canvas:** 1080×1350 (portrait 4:5)
**Visual:** A stark, beautiful photo of a guitar neck backlit by stage lights. Dark, moody. Maybe just a silhouette of a bucket (chicken bucket, obviously) against red-orange stage lights.
**Text overlay (Epilogue 900, large, cream):**
> **400 albums.**
> **One bucket.**
> **Tonight at El Rey.**

**Small footer (Inter 10pt, bottom):** *"→ swipe for why this matters"*

**Why it works:** The cover is a tease, not a sell. "400 albums. One bucket." is a Wait-what? hook. People swipe.

---

### Slide 2 — The Setup

**Visual:** Cream solid background #fbf7f1, full-bleed.
**Text (Inter 18pt, centered, dark charcoal #1a1614):**
> If you've never heard of Buckethead, here's what you're missing:
>
> He wears a plain white mask and an empty KFC bucket on his head.
>
> He plays a Les Paul faster than any guitarist alive.
>
> He's released more studio albums than The Beatles, Bob Dylan, and Taylor Swift combined — by a factor of five.
>
> He's playing a 400-seat room in Downtown Albuquerque tonight.

**No graphic. No emoji. Just text. It's a slide, not a poster.**

---

### Slide 3 — The Stat Card

**Visual:** Terra #9a442d solid background.
**Large Epilogue 900 white text, center:**
> **400+**
**Inter below:**
> studio albums released since 1996
>
> For reference:
> • Bob Dylan: 40
> • The Beatles: 13
> • Taylor Swift: 11

**Small italic footer:**
> *one of the most prolific musicians ever to walk into a venue.*

**Why it works:** People screenshot stat cards. And the comparison makes the number hit.

---

### Slide 4 — The "Will I Like It?" Check

**Visual:** Split card, 50/50 left-right.
**Left side (sage green #4f6249 bg, cream text):**
> **You'll love it if**
> • you like guitars played fast
> • you like weird
> • you've ever said "I don't care who he opens for, I'm going"

**Right side (cream bg, dark text):**
> **Skip it if**
> • you prefer lyrics to shredding
> • you don't like masks (he never takes his off)
> • you have work in the morning (show goes late)

**Why it works:** It gives people permission to self-sort. Counter-intuitively, accounts that tell you *not* to go to things build more trust — you learn their taste.

---

### Slide 5 — The Action

**Visual:** Photo of El Rey Theater exterior at dusk with the marquee lit. Slightly moody, warm.
**Terra banner across bottom (Epilogue 900 cream text):**
> **Tonight at El Rey.**
> **Doors 7. Show 8.**
> **Link in bio.**

**Tiny logo bottom right (Zia magnifying glass).**

---

## Caption (short — the carousel IS the content)

```
Tonight's that one. Buckethead at El Rey.

Full story in the slides ↑

📍 El Rey Theater · Downtown ABQ
🎟️ Link in bio

#Buckethead #ElRey #Albuquerque #DowntownABQ #ABQshows
```

**Why short:** The carousel already said everything. Caption is just the CTA + hashtags.

---

## What this style is trading

- ✅ **Highest engagement format in 2024.** People swipe → dwell time → algorithm reward → more reach
- ✅ **Saves like crazy.** People save carousels to reference later — for events, this is gold.
- ✅ **Room for nuance.** You can teach, sell, and warn all in one post.
- ✅ **Repeatable template.** Once you have the 5-slide structure, any event can slot into it: Cover / Setup / Stat / "Will I like it?" / Action
- ❌ **Time-expensive.** 45–60 min per post even with a template.
- ❌ **Overkill for small events.** A library storytime doesn't need 5 slides.

---

## When this style wins

- Events where name recognition is low but the story is compelling (the Buckethead case exactly)
- Weekend roundups ("5 things to do this weekend" — 1 cover + 5 event slides + 1 action)
- "Venue of the month" deep-dives
- Mission/brand posts (1: the problem, 2: the stat, 3: the solution, 4: what we do, 5: link)

## When this style loses

- Tonight-only urgency (by the time someone swipes through 5 slides, the show's halfway over)
- Free/community events (overproduced for the vibe)

---

## Cadence fit

**1–2 posts/week.** These are tentpole posts — they anchor the feed, then example 1 (poster) and example 2 (photo-forward) fill the mid-week rhythm.

---

## The template is the point

The biggest value of this style isn't any single post — it's that **the 5-slide structure becomes your brand's visual signature.** Once people see 3–4 of these in a row, they recognize the account instantly. That's real compounding.

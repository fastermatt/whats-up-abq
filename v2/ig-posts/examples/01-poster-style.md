# Example 1 — Typographic Poster

**Style:** Text does the heavy lifting. Image is a mood, not a subject. The first-line hook is *the entire post*.

**Best if:** You want the account to feel like a design-forward magazine (think: @eater headlines, @dothebay feed). Cheap to produce — can generate in Canva or Figma in 10 minutes per post.

---

## Visual Direction

**Canvas:** 1080×1350 (portrait 4:5 — IG's most-real-estate format)

**Layers (top to bottom):**
1. **Full-bleed background photo**: moody, low-light shot of El Rey Theater marquee at night, or just a dark silhouette with stage lights. Desaturated/high-contrast. Almost abstract.
2. **Subtle dark gradient overlay** on top half for text legibility
3. **Headline text (Epilogue 900)**, left-aligned, 48–56pt white or cream:
   > **TONIGHT**
   > **BUCKETHEAD**
   > **AT EL REY.**
   > **THAT'S IT.**
   > **THAT'S THE POST.**
4. **Small bottom-left caption strip** (Inter 14pt, cream at 60% opacity):
   > APR 17 · EL REY THEATER · DOWNTOWN ABQ · LINK IN BIO
5. **Tiny logo** (Zia magnifying glass) in bottom right corner, 40×40px, 40% opacity

**Color palette:**
- Background: desaturated photo (or #1a1614 solid black with stage-light highlights)
- Text: cream #fbf7f1
- Accent: terra #9a442d (used on "TONIGHT" word only)

**Why it works:** The headline IS the caption. Someone scrolling IG on mute, at 2x speed, in 0.8 seconds, gets the joke and the info. You lose nothing if they don't read the caption below.

---

## Caption (posted below the image)

```
Buckethead. El Rey. Tonight.

400 studio albums. Faster than physics. Chicken bucket.
An American treasure playing a 400-seat room in Downtown ABQ.

Doors 7 · Show 8 · Link in bio

#Buckethead #ElRey #Albuquerque #DowntownABQ #ABQmusic #BurqueShows
```

**Caption length:** 39 words. Designed to NOT compete with the image — it just backs it up.

---

## What this style is trading

- ✅ **Saves well.** Cleanly-designed poster-style posts have the highest save rate per @later.com 2024 IG report (~2.3x vs photo-only)
- ✅ **Screenshots well.** Someone can share your poster in a group text and all the info is in the image
- ✅ **Cheap to produce.** 10 min in Canva from a template. One person can do 5/week.
- ❌ **Impersonal.** Doesn't feel like "a human is behind this." May not build parasocial connection.
- ❌ **Homogeneous feed risk.** If every post is the same template, the grid feels robotic.

---

## When this style wins

- Posts about ticketed, big-name events where name recognition is the whole game
- Tonight / Tomorrow / This Weekend urgency posts
- "One thing, clearly" moments (vs. roundups)

## When this style loses

- Community/free events (needs warmth, not stark poster vibes)
- Mission/brand posts (the "hey, here's what this account is" posts — too cold)
- Carousel-friendly subjects (lists, roundups, "top 5")

---

## Cadence fit

**3–4 posts/week possible** once you have a Canva template. Every Tuesday/Thursday evening → auto-generate from the upcoming-event list, swap image + date + name, done in 5 minutes.

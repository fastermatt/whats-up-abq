# ABQ Unplugged — Product Context

## Product Purpose
An events aggregator and discovery tool for Albuquerque, NM. Makes it dead simple to find something to do — tonight, this weekend, or any time. Aggregates 1,000+ events from Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, Eventbrite, NHCC, and local/volunteer sources. Updated daily.

## Register
product

## Users

### Primary: The ABQ Resident Who Doesn't Know What to Do
- Albuquerque locals (not tourists) aged 25–55
- Typically mobile, often Friday/Saturday evening decision-makers
- Looking for something specific ("live music tonight") or browsing broadly ("what's happening this weekend")
- Frustrated by Eventbrite (requires login), Google's event carousel (incomplete), and Facebook Events (algorithm-buried)
- Core need: find an event, decide in under 60 seconds, get the ticket link

### Secondary: The Planner
- Plans ahead (1–2 weeks), might be organizing for a group
- Uses filters: date, category, neighborhood, free/paid
- Values completeness — wants to see everything, not just sponsored events

### Tertiary: The Newcomer / Tourist
- Just moved to Albuquerque or visiting
- Doesn't know venue names or neighborhoods
- Needs editorial help: "what's worth going to?"

## Brand Voice
Direct, local, unsentimental. A knowledgeable friend who lives in ABQ and tells you exactly where to go on a Wednesday night. Not a tourism board. Not a press release. Short sentences. Personal voice. No corporate warmth.

Anti-references:
- Eventbrite: transactional, requires account, algorithm-driven
- Facebook Events: algorithm-buried, noisy
- TripAdvisor: tourist-optimized, review-heavy, not event-forward
- Visitalbuquerque.org: official/sterile, not updated frequently enough

## Strategic Principles
1. Events first — the event card is everything. Get out of the way.
2. Speed over features — user wants an answer in 30 seconds, not a profile page
3. Zero friction — no required login to browse, minimal steps to save an event
4. Local authority — links to official venues, external trust signals, no fake reviews
5. SEO as product — landing pages that rank for time-sensitive queries ("tonight", "this weekend", "free") drive new users organically

# ABQ Unplugged — Launch Playbook

Everything you need to start driving traffic, ranking in search, and keeping the site fresh. Read top-to-bottom once, then use as a reference.

**Site:** https://abqunplugged.com
**Goal:** Be the default "what's happening in ABQ?" answer for every Burqueño between 25 and 55 by end of 2026.

---

## 0. Before you spend a dollar on ads — 3 launch-readiness fixes

1. **Add an email capture above the fold.** The weekly digest infrastructure is built; the UI ask isn't. A compact "Get the Thursday drop — [email] [Subscribe]" below the hero stats converts bouncers into subscribers for life.
2. **Add visible social proof in the first viewport.** "Trusted by N Burqueños" (or venue logo strip: "Events from Popejoy · Revel · KiMo · Launchpad · Isotopes Park") answers the "is this legit?" question social visitors are asking.
3. **Add save-this-event hearts on the card grid, not just the detail page.** Low-friction interactions convert skeptical visitors into returning users.

Without these three, every Instagram click has ~80% chance of bouncing forever. **Spend one day on these before launching campaigns.**

## 1. Social media strategy

### Pick your channels — not all five

| Channel | Use it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram (grid + stories + reels)** | ✅ **Primary** | Visual-first, high engagement in 25-55 local audience. ABQ community is active here. |
| **Facebook Page + Events** | ✅ **Primary** | Still where Burqueño 35+ actually plans their weekend. Cross-post from IG + use native Events cross-referencing for SEO. |
| **TikTok** | 🟡 **Experimental** | Big upside if one reel hits, but content treadmill is brutal. Try 1 per week; measure before scaling. |
| **Threads / Bluesky** | 🟡 **Low effort, low cost** | Auto-crosspost from IG. Reply to ABQ conversations. Zero dedicated content creation. |
| **Twitter/X** | ⚠️ **Skip for now** | ABQ local community has largely moved. Revisit if API pricing changes. |
| **Reddit (r/Albuquerque)** | ✅ **Critical** | See "backlinks & mentions" section. Read-only participation + 1 strategic post. |
| **Nextdoor** | ✅ **Hidden gem** | Hyperlocal, high-trust, older audience. Post event recaps in each neighborhood. |

### Content pillars (5 per month, rotating)

1. **Tonight / This Weekend** (editorial picks, 2–3 events curated) — **50% of posts**
2. **Hidden gem** (venue, artist, or recurring event people don't know about) — **20% of posts**
3. **Burqueño proud** (local photography, neighborhood spotlights, "why we love ABQ") — **15% of posts**
4. **Use-the-site** (how to set preferences, save events, thumbs-down, the Thursday drop) — **10% of posts**
5. **Event recaps** (quick photo dumps from events that happened) — **5% of posts**

### Cadence — realistic, not heroic

| Platform | Posting rhythm | Content source |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed | **3/week** (Mon, Thu, Sat) | Use the IG Editor at `/ig-editor.html` — auto-fills from any event URL |
| Instagram stories | **5/week** | Cross-post + quick polls ("comedy or ballet this weekend?") |
| Instagram reels | **1/week** (Friday) | Balloon-over-Sandias time-lapse, event crowds, venue reveals |
| Facebook | Auto-crosspost from IG | Zero extra effort |
| Thursday email | **1/week** (Thu 9am) | Already automated — `send-digest.mjs` |
| Reddit r/Albuquerque | **1/month max** | High-value posts only (see section 6) |

### Posting rules (cheap but critical)

- **No generic stock photos.** Every post uses a real ABQ photo OR the IG Editor template (which pulls the event's own image).
- **Location tag every post** to the venue (not just "Albuquerque, NM").
- **Tag the venue** when the event has a public IG handle. Popejoy, NHCC, Revel, KiMo all repost.
- **First comment has the link** (IG kills reach on link-in-caption posts).
- **Post between 6–9pm local time** for evening events; 8–10am for day events.

## 3. SEO — current state + priority fixes

### Current state (good)
- ✅ Sitemap live at `/sitemap.xml` with **1,110 URLs**, fresh lastmod timestamps
- ✅ Per-event Open Graph tags + Schema.org Event JSON-LD on every event page
- ✅ BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on event / category / venue pages
- ✅ Category landing pages (Music, Sports, Comedy, Family, Arts & Theater, Food & Drink, Film, Community, Festivals, Outdoor) — all returning 200
- ✅ Venue + neighborhood landing pages
- ✅ Robots.txt allows all crawling, disallows `/api/` and `/_next/`
- ✅ HTTPS + HSTS + nosniff headers

### Top 5 SEO fixes (weekend project)

**1. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster**
If you haven't already: claim the property at https://search.google.com/search-console, add the sitemap URL, and request indexing of the homepage + `/events`. This alone gets you into search within 1–2 weeks.

**2. Fix the homepage Cache-Control (cache-miss storm right now)**
Your `netlify.toml` sets `public, s-maxage=60, stale-while-revalidate=600` but the homepage serves `private, no-cache, no-store` (Next.js override). Check `v2/app/page.tsx` — if `export const revalidate = 0` anywhere, change to `60`. This alone can 5x your Lighthouse score on the homepage.

**3. Per-category SEO titles that target actual search queries**
Audit `/categories/[slug]/page.tsx` titles. Rewrite as:
- `Live Music in Albuquerque — Concerts This Week | ABQ Unplugged`
- `Comedy Shows in Albuquerque | ABQ Unplugged`
- `Family Events in Albuquerque — Kids-Friendly Things to Do | ABQ Unplugged`
- `Things to Do in Nob Hill Albuquerque | ABQ Unplugged` (neighborhoods)

The goal: rank #1 for "things to do in [neighborhood]" queries. These are low-competition.

**4. Build "Tonight" and "This Weekend" server-rendered content**
Your `/tonight` and `/weekend` pages are gold — every Thursday evening someone in ABQ searches "[date] albuquerque events" and these pages should answer that. Add:
- Top 5 events with 1-sentence editorial blurb (use LM Studio to auto-draft)
- Weather forecast context (call OpenWeather free API)
- "Last updated: [time]" stamp so Google understands freshness

**5. Local business schema on the homepage**
Add a single `@type: LocalBusiness` JSON-LD block to the homepage with NAP (Name, Address = PO box or general ABQ, Phone optional). This is separate from Event schema and helps Google understand you're an ABQ-local resource.

## 5. Paid strategy (when you're ready)

**Don't start here.** Organic + email + Reddit can get you to 5K monthly visits for $0. When you're ready:

### Where to spend first
1. **Instagram Boost on top-3 posts** — $5–10/post. Objective: Reach + profile visits. Targeting: Albuquerque + 25-mile radius, age 25-55, interests: live music, theater, local events.
2. **Meta Lead Ads** (IG or FB) — low-cost email capture ads promoting the Thursday drop. ~$2-4/lead is achievable in a local market.
3. **Google Search** — only after organic indexing is in place. Bid on low-competition queries like "things to do albuquerque tonight," "free events albuquerque."

**Don't spend on TikTok ads, Twitter, LinkedIn, or display networks** for this audience and budget. Waste.

### Realistic budget ramp
| Month | Spend | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0 | Launch, pre-SEO, organic Reddit/Nextdoor |
| 2 | $100 | Boost the 3 best-performing organic posts |
| 3 | $200 | Boost + Meta Lead Ads for email capture |
| 4–6 | $300–500/mo | Add Google Search for decision-intent queries |

## 7. Success metrics — what to track

### Vanity metrics (weekly check-in)
- IG follower count
- Homepage visits (Umami dashboard — site ID `66fc7cf5`)
- Email subscribers (count of `user_email_prefs` where `opted_in = true`)

### Real metrics (monthly)
- **Return visitor rate** — Umami `Returning visitors` chart. Target: 30%+ by month 3.
- **Email open rate** — Resend dashboard. Target: 35%+ (local newsletters beat the 22% avg).
- **Event detail conversions** — events marked "Going" / saves per month. Target: 200 by month 3.
- **Referral sources** — which platforms drive traffic. Expect: direct > IG > Google > Reddit > others.

### Mission-critical metrics (quarterly)
- **Saved events per user** (median) — proves people are using it, not just browsing
- **Digests opened → event clicked** — proves the matcher is actually matching well
- **Organic search visits** — proves SEO work is paying off
